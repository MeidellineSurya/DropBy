Concept Summary — Location-Based Social Deal Discovery App
Core Idea
A location-based discovery app inspired by Pokémon GO, where users explore their city to find limited-time deals on food, activities, and experiences.
Unlike a normal deals app, users cannot simply browse every offer from home. Deals only appear when users are physically near a participating location, creating a sense of exploration, rarity, urgency, and discovery.
Some offers can only be redeemed when the user brings enough people, turning discounts into spontaneous social experiences.
The concept can be summarised as:
“Pokémon GO, but you catch experiences.”
________________


How It Works
Businesses with unused capacity can release temporary Drops.
For example:
* 40% off Korean BBQ for groups of 4+
* Half-price bowling for 3–6 people
* $10 karaoke for groups of 5+
* Free dessert with a group meal
* Discounted escape-room session
* Limited café or restaurant specials
Instead of immediately showing the exact business and discount, users initially see mysterious Drops nearby.
For example:
🎁 Rare Food Drop — 300m away
The closer the user gets, the more information becomes available.
This makes finding the deal part of the experience.
________________


Deal Types
Solo Drop
Can be redeemed individually.
Example:
Free coffee upgrade
Squad Drop
Requires a small group.
Example:
40% off Korean BBQ — minimum 4 people
Raid Drop
Requires a larger group.
Example:
$100 karaoke room → $30 — requires 6 people
Users can invite friends or potentially open their group to other nearby users who want to join.
________________


Discovery System
Deals are progressively revealed depending on proximity.
Stage 1 — Detect
A Drop appears somewhere nearby.
🔥 Drop detected — 700m
Only approximate information is shown.
Stage 2 — Reveal
When the user gets closer:
🍜 Food Drop
Rare
180m
Stage 3 — Discover
At close range, the full deal is revealed.
40% off Korean BBQ
Minimum 4 people
Expires in 28 minutes
Stage 4 — Assemble
The user creates or joins a group.
2/4 people ready
They can invite friends or potentially allow nearby users to request a spot.
Stage 5 — Claim
The required group arrives and checks in at the venue.
The deal becomes active and can be redeemed.
________________


User Workflow
1. Open App
The user opens a live map showing nearby unidentified Drops.
Example:
🎁 250m
🔥 600m
👥 430m
✨ 1.1km
The exact businesses are hidden.
↓
2. Choose a Drop
The user notices a Drop that looks interesting.
✨ Rare Activity Drop — 400m
They begin walking toward it.
↓
3. Get Closer
As the user approaches, more information appears.
🎳 Activity
Rare
120m
↓
4. Discover the Deal
At close range:
Strike Bowling
50% off one game
👥 Requires 4 people
⏱ 26 minutes remaining
↓
5. Assemble a Group
The user can:
* Invite friends
* Share the Drop
* Open the group to nearby users
Example:
Squad: 2/4
↓
6. Group Confirmed
Once enough people join:
4/4 — Squad Ready
Everyone receives directions to the venue.
↓
7. Check In
Users arrive and confirm their presence through a QR code, geofence, or venue code.
↓
8. Redeem
The business confirms the Drop.
🎉 DROP COMPLETED
Users receive the discount or experience.
↓
9. Progression
Completing Drops can provide:
* XP
* achievements
* collectible badges
* exploration statistics
* rare Drop access

* group rewards
↓
10. Keep Exploring
After completing one Drop, another may appear nearby.
This creates the main engagement loop:
Explore → Detect → Discover → Assemble → Experience → Reward → Explore again

Business Workflow
1. Business Has Spare Capacity
Example:
A restaurant has many empty tables between 3–5 PM.
↓
2. Create a Drop
The business selects:
* Offer
* Number of customers required
* Maximum users
* Duration
* Discovery radius
Example:
35% off food
Minimum: 4 people
Maximum: 8 people
Duration: 45 minutes
Radius: 800m
↓
3. Drop Appears
Nearby users begin seeing the unidentified Drop.
↓
4. Users Discover It
Interested users travel toward the venue and assemble a group.
↓
5. Customers Arrive
Instead of attracting one discounted customer, the business may attract an entire group.
↓
6. Deal Expires
Once capacity is reached or the time limit ends, the Drop disappears automatically.
________________


Why Businesses Would Use It
Traditional discounts can reduce prices for customers who may have visited anyway.
This system allows businesses to release discounts only when they actually need customers.
It helps convert:
unused capacity → immediate demand
The group mechanic provides another advantage.
Instead of:
1 user discovers deal → 1 customer
the app encourages:
1 user discovers deal → brings 3–6 customers
________________


Gamification
Drops could have rarity levels:
Common
Small rewards or discounts
Uncommon
Good everyday deals
Rare
Large discounts or attractive experiences
Epic
Very limited, high-value offers
Legendary
Extremely rare experiences or major discounts
Seeing:
🌟 LEGENDARY DROP — 600m
creates curiosity and encourages exploration.
________________


Longer-Term Features
Combo Drops
Completing several experiences during the same outing could unlock bonuses.
Example:
Dinner Drop completed
Activity Drop completed
🔓 Night Out Chest
Complete one more Drop to unlock a reward.
City Events
Businesses or sponsors could create city-wide treasure hunts.
Example:
10 hidden Drops across Melbourne CBD today.
Social Squads
Users can create regular groups with friends and build shared achievements.
Open Squads
Users who need additional people can allow nearby users to join their experience.
________________


Core Value Proposition
For Users
Discover cheap and interesting things to do while exploring the city and spending time with other people.
For Businesses
Quickly fill unused capacity by releasing temporary deals that encourage customers to arrive in groups.
Main Difference From Existing Deals Apps
Instead of:
Open app → browse discounts → choose restaurant
the experience becomes:
Explore → find something mysterious → discover the offer → gather people → experience it together
The deal itself becomes part of a real-world game.
